If your OnePlus 13R battery drains faster than usual, takes too long to charge, or gets unusually warm during everyday use, it may be time to check its battery health. These symptoms may indicate OnePlus 13R battery charging issues. Background apps, charging accessories, software bugs, and battery wear can cause the issue.
